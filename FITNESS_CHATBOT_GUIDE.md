## 🎯 How to Use

### Access the Chatbot
1. Look for the **floating chat bubble** in the bottom-right corner
2. Click to open the chat window
3. Type your question and press Enter or click Send

### Best Practices
- **Be specific**: "How do I do squats?" vs "Exercise help"
- **Ask follow-ups**: The bot can provide more details
- **Use natural language**: Talk like you would to a real coach

### Sample Conversations

**Example 1: Exercise Form**
```
You: How do I perform a proper deadlift?
Bot: [Detailed step-by-step instructions with tips]
You: What if my back hurts?
Bot: [Safety advice and form corrections]
```

**Example 2: Workout Planning**
```
You: Give me a 20-minute home workout
Bot: [Complete workout routine with exercises]
You: Can I do this every day?
Bot: [Recovery and frequency advice]
```

**Example 3: Nutrition**
```
You: What supplements should I take?
Bot: [Beginner supplement guide]
You: When should I take protein powder?
Bot: [Timing and dosage recommendations]
```

## 💡 Tips for Users

### Getting the Best Answers
1. **Start broad, then narrow**: "Tell me about squats" → "How deep should I squat?"
2. **Mention your level**: "I'm a beginner" helps tailor advice
3. **State your goal**: "I want to build muscle" or "I want to lose weight"
4. **Ask for alternatives**: "I can't do pushups, what else?"

### Common Use Cases
- **Before workout**: "What's a good warmup?"
- **During workout**: "How many reps should I do?"
- **After workout**: "What stretches should I do?"
- **Planning**: "Create a weekly workout schedule"
- **Shopping**: "What supplements do I need?"
- **Troubleshooting**: "Why am I not seeing results?"
