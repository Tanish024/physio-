# YouTube Pose Capture Feature - User Guide

## 🎯 What It Does

Capture trainer poses directly from YouTube workout videos and get real-time feedback on how well you're matching them!

## 🚀 How to Use

### Step 1: Load a YouTube Video
1. Go to the Pose Match page
2. Paste any YouTube workout video URL
3. Click "Load Video"

### Step 2: Find the Perfect Pose
1. Play the video
2. Pause at the trainer's pose you want to match
3. Make sure the trainer is clearly visible

### Step 3: Capture the Trainer's Pose
1. Click the green **"📸 Capture Trainer Pose"** button
2. Your browser will ask "Which screen to capture?"
3. Select the browser tab with the YouTube video
4. Click "Share"

### Step 4: Match the Pose
1. Enable your webcam
2. Try to match the trainer's pose
3. See real-time accuracy scores:
   - **Overall Accuracy** (0-100%)
   - **Upper Body** accuracy
   - **Lower Body** accuracy
   - **Core** accuracy

## 🎨 Visual Feedback

- **Green Skeleton** = Your pose (from webcam)
- **Blue Skeleton** = Trainer's pose (from captured video)
- **Accuracy Meters** = How well you match

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Good Lighting**: Make sure both you and the trainer video are well-lit
2. **Full Body Visible**: Ensure the entire body is in frame
3. **Clear Pose**: Pause at a clear, stable pose (not mid-movement)
4. **Camera Position**: Position your webcam to capture your full body
5. **Try Multiple Angles**: Capture different poses from the same video

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "No pose detected in captured frame"
- The trainer might not be clearly visible
- Try pausing at a different moment
- Make sure the trainer's full body is in the video frame

### "Failed to capture pose"
- Make sure you selected the correct browser tab
- Try again and ensure you click "Share" when prompted
- Check that screen capture permissions are enabled

### Accuracy seems off
- Make sure your webcam can see your full body
- Adjust lighting in your room
- Try to match the exact angle of the trainer

## 🎓 Exercise Types Supported

- **Plank** - Core stability
- **Squat** - Lower body strength
- **Push-up** - Upper body strength
- **Yoga** - Flexibility and balance
- **Other** - General exercises

## 🔒 Privacy

- Screen capture happens locally in your browser
- No video data is sent to any server
- Pose detection runs entirely on your device
- You control what you capture and when

## 📊 Understanding Accuracy Scores

- **90-100%**: Excellent form! 🎉
- **75-89%**: Good form, minor adjustments needed
- **60-74%**: Decent form, some improvements needed
- **Below 60%**: Keep practicing, focus on alignment

## 🎯 Best Practices

1. **Warm up** before attempting poses
2. **Start with easier poses** and progress gradually
3. **Focus on form** over speed
4. **Use the feedback** to improve your technique
5. **Practice regularly** for best results
